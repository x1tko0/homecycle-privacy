HomeCycle

Maintenance Tracker — Privacy Policy

Short version

Your home data stays on your phone. HomeCycle has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not request the Internet, camera or media-storage permissions — the catalog of items, rooms and tasks is built into the app.

Data the app stores

Only what you enter yourself:

  • Items you maintain and rooms you clean, with their tasks and intervals.
  • Item passport details you type in: model, serial number, purchase and warranty dates, store, spare part, notes.
  • Journal: when you completed, skipped or postponed a task, optional cost and notes.
  • Photos you take or pick (receipts, labels, manuals).
  • Household member names you add, “I’m away” dates and app settings (hemisphere, reminders, theme, sounds).

This data:

  • is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage;
  • is never sent to the developer or any third party;
  • is deleted with “Delete all data” in Settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Photos

When you add a photo, Android opens its own camera app or the system photo picker. HomeCycle receives only the single photo you take or choose and keeps a copy in its private storage. The app never browses your gallery and does not have camera or media permissions.

Seasons

To time seasonal tasks (like cleaning the air conditioner before summer), HomeCycle guesses your hemisphere from the time zone set on your phone. It does not use location. You can change it in Settings.

Backups

  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include HomeCycle's data and settings (without photos) in your device backup to your Google account and restore them on a new phone. This is controlled by Android and Google (Google Privacy Policy), not by us, and you can turn it off in system settings.
  • Backup file and CSV. “Create backup” (a ZIP with your photos) and “Export journal” create a file only where you choose to save it. We never see it.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information, accounts or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, or access to your gallery or files (except a photo or backup file you pick).
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash-reporting SDKs or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising.

Google Play services

The app talks only to the Google Play Store app on your phone:

  • Google Play Billing — the one-time “HomeCycle Pro” purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details.
  • In-app updates and in-app review, provided by Google Play.

Notifications

The morning summary, the evening nudge, warranty reminders, milestones and the trial reminder are scheduled on your device. Notification text (item and task names) can appear on your lock screen — you can turn any of them off in Settings.

Sharing

An item passport, shopping list or household plan is created on your device and shared only when you tap “Share” and choose where to send it.

Children

HomeCycle is a general-audience app. It collects no data from anyone, including children.
